# Haunted Boardwalk Art Style Concepts

Concept images for an animated kids TV show about kids running a haunted boardwalk / fairgrounds, with help from undead/ghosts/ghouls. They compete in Spookball and other living-vs-dead contests, and the monsters help with problems.

## Art Styles Explored

### Risograph / Limited-Ink Screenprint Festival-Poster Style
- Bright, graphic, grainy texture with hot pink, fluorescent orange, teal, black
- Living carnival poster aesthetic

### Bright Art Deco Geometric Elegance
- 1920s-30s luxury poster style
- Strong symmetry, chevrons, sunbursts, gold/cream/black with jewel tones
- Elegant, glamorous undead helpers

Images are in the /images/ directory.
